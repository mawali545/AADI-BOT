DR Candle Tracker Android build 197

Standalone package: com.dr.candletracker
No AADI signal engine or AADI dashboard is used.
Quant scan: last 8 completed real 10-second candles; normalized candle-body votes; close-to-close momentum; directional agreement gate.
Accuracy: session-only outcome tracking, resolved against the close of the next real 10-second candle. Ties and unavailable outcomes are excluded from the win-rate denominator.
Market data: explicit price fields paired with a recognized market asset only. Unknown/binary frames are not guessed.
No synthetic ticks, sample candles, automatic trades, or guaranteed accuracy.
